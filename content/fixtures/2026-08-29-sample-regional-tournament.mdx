## NEWS

**Fixture content.** This event did not take place.

In the fictional scenario, a regional qualifier finished with both finalists on
identical scores across all three picks, sending the match to a random-select
tiebreak that neither player had practised.[^1]

## CONTEXT

Tiebreak rules are usually the least-discussed part of a ruleset and the part
most likely to decide a title. A random select rewards breadth; a fixed
tiebreak chart rewards preparation. Neither is wrong, but a scene should decide
which one it wants before it needs it, not during a final.

## PLAYER IMPACT

Practically: if you are entering brackets this season, read the tiebreak clause
before you build a practice list.

Structurally, this is the kind of edge case that pushes organisers to publish
rulesets earlier — which is good for everyone, and the sort of change STEPWIRE
intends to keep a record of.
