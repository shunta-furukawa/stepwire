## NEWS

**Fixture content.** The pack described below is invented for development
purposes.

Six songs arrive in a fictional seasonal pack.[^1] Five sit in the middle of the
difficulty table. The sixth holds 300 BPM through its entire second half, which
would make it the fastest sustained chart of the fictional season.

## CONTEXT

Sustained tempo and peak tempo are different problems. A chart that touches
300 BPM for eight beats is a spike; a chart that holds it for ninety seconds is
a stamina test, and stamina tests age differently in a community — they stay
relevant for years because they cannot be brute-forced in an afternoon.

## PLAYER IMPACT

If you are working through the upper mid-table, five of these six are ordinary
additions to a rotation.

The fast chart is the one to plan around. Charts of that shape reward footwork
efficiency over reaction speed, so the useful preparation is a slower chart with
the same step pattern rather than a faster chart with a different one.
